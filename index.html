Questions, answered

FAQ

What is DateMe?

DateMe: Rank Your Photos is an iPhone app that scores your real dating photos for how they land on a profile, picks the photo you should lead with, and shows the one fix that helps each photo most. It also drafts dating-app bios in your own voice. It’s built by MHND LABS.

Does DateMe upload my photos anywhere?

No. Analysis runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device models. Your photos never leave the phone, there’s no account to create, and the app’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

Which iPhones does DateMe work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later can rank photos. The deeper AI coaching — building your best profile and drafting bios — uses Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

Is DateMe free?

The public beta is free on TestFlight today. When DateMe reaches the App Store, photo scoring stays free; the full coaching — per-photo fixes, profile building, and bios — is part of DateMe Pro.

Will DateMe swipe or message for me?

No. DateMe is a coach, not automation. It never swipes, never sends messages, and never posts on your behalf — it helps you show up as yourself, better.

Does DateMe generate AI photos of me?

No. DateMe works only with photos you already have. It never generates, retouches, or invents faces — the person who shows up to the date should be the person in the profile.
