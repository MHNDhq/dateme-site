Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

DateMe is built around one promise: your photos never leave your iPhone.

The short version

Your photos, and the face details the app reads from them, are analyzed entirely on your iPhone and are never uploaded anywhere. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising, and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device relates to purchases, if you subscribe (see below).

Photos

  • The photos you add are analyzed entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device intelligence.
  • Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and are never sent off the device.
  • Photos and their analysis are stored locally on your device, and are removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Information we collect

DateMe does not collect personal information, usage analytics, contact information, or location data. The one exception is purchase information when you subscribe to DateMe Pro, described below.

Purchases (DateMe Pro)

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. To know which features you've unlocked, DateMe uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, which receives your purchase history tied to a random, anonymous identifier (not your name, email, or Apple ID). This is used only to activate your subscription and to show us aggregate sales analytics. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Your photos and face data are never shared with RevenueCat or anyone else. To request deletion of purchase records, email us at the address below.

Third parties

DateMe does not include advertising or tracking SDKs. Apart from Apple (payments) and RevenueCat (purchase history, described above), no data is shared with third parties.

On-device AI

DateMe uses Apple's on-device foundation models to analyze photos and draft text. This processing runs on your device; DateMe does not send your content to our servers (we don't operate any). If a future version uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute for some processing, that is designed by Apple so your content is not accessible to Apple or to us — and this policy will be updated to say so.

Children

DateMe is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
