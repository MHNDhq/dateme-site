Requirements

DateMe needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer running iOS 27, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). On unsupported devices, the on-device analysis won't run.

Quick start

  1. Open DateMe and add 5–9 of your real dating photos.
  2. Each photo is scored privately on your device. The ⭐ marks your strongest "lead" photo.
  3. Tap a photo to see what's working and the one change that helps most.
  4. Add "Your voice," then tap Build best profile for the recommended order and bios in your voice.

FAQ

Is it private?

Yes. Your photos are analyzed entirely on your iPhone and never leave the device. No account, no uploads.

Does it work offline?

Yes — analysis runs on-device.

Does it post, swipe, or message for me?

No. DateMe is a coach: it never swipes, messages, or posts. You stay in control.

Does it use AI to fake my photos?

Never. DateMe only works with your real photos — no AI-generated faces.

Why is one of my photos scored low?

Common reasons: face partly obscured, closed eyes, soft focus, harsh lighting, or a busy background. Tap the photo for the specific fix.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your device. Remove a photo in the app, or delete the app, and its data is gone.
